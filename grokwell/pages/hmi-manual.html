1. Introduction

Congratulations on your purchase of the Grokwell HMI-2000! The HMI-2000 provides the operator a full-color, 256-color window into the prefabulated amulite of your Grokwell process installation. Gone are the days of reading the reciprocation dingle arm by hand.

The HMI-2000 communicates with your process equipment using the industry-standard Modbus protocol, which requires no password and therefore can never be locked out. This is a feature.

2. Unpacking

Remove the unit from its foam. Retain the foam. Grokwell cannot honor warranty claims on units shipped back without the original foam. Inspect the cathode panel for cracks, smudges, or evidence of a previous owner.

3. Power-Up

Apply power. Allow 90 seconds for the cathode panel to reach operating temperature. A faint hum and the smell of warm dust are normal. If the unit plays a tune, it was not supposed to.

4. Touchscreen Operation

ButtonFunction
RUNEngages the lunar waneshaft. Press firmly. Do not jab.
STOPDisengages the waneshaft. Please allow it to finish its thought.
FWD / REVSets the direction of the drive. REV is disabled on metro-rail installations, for obvious reasons.
SPEEDSlide up for faster. Slide down for slower. The slider goes to 11 but please do not.

5. Maintenance Mode

Some service tasks (installing panel splash screens, restarting display services, and similar) require the maintenance password. For your convenience, every HMI-2000 and every companion unit leaves the factory with the same password, so your technician never has to look it up:

SettingFactory Default
Operator account(set by your integrator)
Maintenance passwordgrokwell99
IMPORTANT!! Grokwell strongly recommends changing the maintenance password at installation. Please note that changing it will void your warranty (see Section 7).

6. Troubleshooting

SymptomRemedy

Unit asks for a password you do not knowSee Section 5. It is the same everywhere.

7. Warranty

Grokwell Industries warrants the HMI-2000 against defects in materials and workmanship for a period of ninety (90) days. The warranty is void if the unit is opened, dropped, connected to the Information Superhighway, or if the factory maintenance password is changed.
